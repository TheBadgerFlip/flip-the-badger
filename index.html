📖 The Official Rules

Etiquette, strategy, and standards of play

🎯 The Objective

Be the first player to reach exactly 15 points by flipping the Badger box and adding the score shown on the upward-facing side. Strategy lies in knowing when to stop and when to press your luck.

✅ The Scoring

  • 👑 Top face up = 10 points — the ultimate prize
  • 📏 Short end up = 5 points — steady progress
  • 🦡 Long side up = 1 point — safe and sure
  • ❌ Bottom face up = 0 points — all turn points forfeit, score remains unchanged

⭐ Basic Play

  • Play on any flat, dry surface with a defined edge
  • The Badger must complete at least one full rotation in the air for the flip to count
  • Continue flipping until you choose to end your turn or flip a zero
  • Reach exactly 15 points = VICTORY!

⭐⭐ Intermediate Play

  • All Basic rules apply
  • The Badger must land a minimum of one box length away from the launch point
  • Falls off the playing surface = zero points, turn ends

⭐⭐⭐ Advanced Play

  • All Intermediate rules apply
  • Announce your current score at the start of every turn
  • Failure to announce = score resets to zero, turn passes immediately
