📖 Game Rules

Objective

Be the first to reach exactly 15 points.

Scoring

  • Top face up = 1 point
  • Bottom face up = 0 points — all points from this turn are lost!
  • Long sides = 5 points each
  • Short ends = 10 points each

Gameplay

Flip the Badger from a flat surface. It must make at least one full rotation. Continue flipping until you decide to pass to the next player.
