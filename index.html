📖 Rules

Objective

Be first to reach exactly 15 points.

Scoring

  • 🦡 Top face = 1 point
  • ❌ Bottom face = 0 points — lose all points from this turn
  • 📏 Long sides = 5 points each
  • 👑 Short ends = 10 points each

Basic Play

  • Play on any flat, dry surface with an edge to flip from
  • The Badger must make at least 1 full rotation
  • Keep flipping until you pass or win
  • Flip a 0 = lose all points from that turn
  • Reach exactly 15 = VICTORY!

Intermediate

  • Must land at least one box length away from flip point
  • Falls off = back to score at start of turn
