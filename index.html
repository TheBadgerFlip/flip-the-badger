📖 How to Play

Complete official rules

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to reach exactly 15 points by flipping the Badger box and scoring points each turn.

✅ Scoring

  • Top face up = 1 point
  • Short end up = 10 points
  • Long side up = 5 points
  • Bottom face up = 0 points — lose all points from this turn!

📋 Basic Rules

  • Play on any flat, dry surface with an edge to flip from
  • The Badger must make at least 1 full rotation when flipped
  • Keep flipping and accumulating points until you decide to stop and pass to the next player
  • If you flip a 0, you lose all points gained this turn and pass with your score unchanged
  • Reach 15 points = YOU WIN!

⭐ Intermediate Rules

  • The Badger must land a minimum of one box length away from where it was flipped
  • If it falls off the playing area = 0 points for that flip
