📖 The Official Rules

Etiquette, strategy, and standards of play

🎯 The Objective

Be the first player to reach exactly 15 points by flipping the Badger box and adding the score shown on the upward-facing side.

✅ The Scoring

  • 👑 Short ends up = 10 points — the ultimate prize
  • 📏 Long sides up = 5 points — steady progress
  • 🦡 Top face up = 1 point — safe and sure
  • ❌ Bottom face up = 0 points — all turn points forfeit, your total remains unchanged

📐 Box Specifications

  • Height: 8.2 cm | Width: 5.5 cm | Depth: 2.2 cm
  • Recommended: 200–250gsm cardstock for perfect balance

⭐ Basic Play

  • Play on any flat, dry surface with a defined edge
  • The Badger must complete at least one full rotation in the air
  • Keep flipping until you choose to pass or win
  • Flip a 0 = lose all points from that turn
  • Reach exactly 15 = VICTORY!

⭐⭐ Intermediate Play

  • All Basic rules apply
  • Must land at least one box length away from flip point
  • Falls off playing area = back to score at start of turn

⭐⭐⭐ Advanced Play

  • All Intermediate rules apply
  • Best of 3 rounds to determine the winner
