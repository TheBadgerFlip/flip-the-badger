📦 Make Your Badger Box

Print, cut, fold — start flipping in minutes!

Box Specifications

  • Dimensions: 8.2cm tall × 5.5cm wide × 2.2cm deep
  • Top face: Badger illustration — 10 points
  • Bottom face: 0 points
  • Long sides: 1 point each
  • Short ends: 5 points each
  • Paper: Cardstock or thick paper recommended

⬇️ Download Printable Template

Ready-to-print PDF — includes folding guides and scoring labels

📄 Download Box Template (PDF)

After downloading: Print → Cut out the shape → Fold along dotted lines → Glue tabs → Start playing!

Assembly Instructions

  1. Print on thick paper or thin card (A4 is fine)
  2. Cut around the outer solid black line
  3. Fold all dotted lines inward before applying glue
  4. Check each face shows the correct score
  5. Glue the small tabs to form a box
  6. Make sure it closes securely — start flipping! 🦡

📖 How to Play

Complete official rules for all levels

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to reach exactly 15 points by flipping the Badger box and adding the score shown on the face that lands upward.

✅ Scoring

  • 🦡 Top face up = 10 points
  • 📏 Short End up = 5 points
  • ➡️ Long Side up = 1 point
  • 🔴 Zero Side up = 0 points — lose all points gained THIS TURN only

🔴 Zero Rule

If the 0 side lands up: You lose all points collected during your current turn, but keep your score from the start of the turn. Your turn ends immediately.

🔄 Bounce-Back Rule

If adding your flip score takes you over 15, you "bounce back" down by the excess amount. Example: 13 + 5 = 18 → 15 − 3 = 12. Your turn ends.

⭐ Basic Mode

  • Play on any flat, dry surface
  • The Badger must make at least 1 full rotation to count
  • Keep flipping until you pass to the next player or win

⭐⭐ Intermediate Mode

  • All Basic rules apply
  • The Badger must land at least 1 full box length away from where it was flipped
  • Lands too close → flip doesn't count, turn ends

⭐⭐⭐ Advanced Mode

  • All Intermediate rules apply
  • At the START of EVERY turn, you must announce your current score
  • Forget to announce → your score resets to 0 and turn passes
  • Falls off the table → reset to 0 and turn passes
